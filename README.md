### 🛰️ About Me

I specialize in **Earth Observation, satellite remote sensing pipelines, and spatiotemporal data modeling**. With 8+ years of experience across Nordic and Indian research institutions, I bridge physical climate sciences with modern data science to transform petabyte-scale planetary data into actionable insights.

- 🔭 **Research:** 8 peer-reviewed publications (including 2 lead-author papers in *JGR: Oceans*) analyzing North Atlantic circulation, Lagrangian particle dispersion, and radar telemetry.
- 💻 **Data Science & ML:** Building automated workflows for optical/radar satellite imagery, time-series forecasting, and agricultural commodity analytics.
- 🛠️ **Tech Stack:** Python (Xarray, GeoPandas, Rasterio, Scikit-learn), Google Earth Engine (GEE), Linux/HPC, and Git.
